# Random Trader — Landing Page

Marketing site for **Random Trader** — a self-hosted AI trading psychology dashboard and journal for prop firm and retail day traders.

Live at [random-trader.com](https://random-trader.com)

---

## What is Random Trader?

Random Trader is a self-hosted AI trading coach that tracks your tilt score, enforces your daily loss limit, and helps you stay consistent — session after session.

- Psychology-first AI mentor with real-time tilt tracking
- Trade journal with emotion and setup quality logging
- Full metrics dashboard — equity curve, win rate, PnL by symbol and hour
- Self-hosted — your data stays on your machine
- One-time payment, no subscription

## Tech

Pure static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS. No framework, no build step. Upload to any web host.
